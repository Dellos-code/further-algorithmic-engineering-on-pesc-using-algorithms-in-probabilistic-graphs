\section*{Regime-adaptive rank fusion}
\subsection*{Method}
The target is expected accessible mass in an uncertain graph. The frozen W1
member is combined in rank space with two-iteration message passing. The
fusion coefficient is a function of graph-only covariates and does not use
ground truth at deployment.

\subsection*{Covariates and evidence}
The primary covariate is mean edge probability over existing edges. Non-edges
are excluded because their absence is not an uncertain-edge observation;
assigning them zero probability would make the statistic depend primarily on
graph order rather than reliability. Additional covariates describe expected
degree, escape probability, spectral radius, non-backtracking growth,
message-passing mass, and expected branching factor. The acquisition search
obtained only \FusionRealNetworkCount{} bundled real networks, so the evidence
mode is \FusionEvidenceMode{} and synthetic held-out panels are primary.

\subsection*{Selection and confirmation}
The pilot and its statistical errata remain immutable evidence. Second-round
fitting uses development data, the no-longer-blind pilot panel, and the larger
fitting panel. Candidate functions and covariates are compared with grouped
cross-validation and the one-standard-error rule; the selected function is
frozen before new held-out computation.

On the fresh test panel, the instance-level mean differences for H1, H2, and
H3 are respectively \FusionHOneMean{}, \FusionHTwoMean{}, and
\FusionHThreeMean{}. Holm-adjusted two-sided probabilities are
\FusionHOneP{}, \FusionHTwoP{}, and \FusionHThreeP{}. These are not claims
about a broad real-network benchmark. Cluster-level intervals and larger
in-family and out-of-family panels are reported separately.

\subsection*{Limitations}
The real-network sample is small, synthetic generators may not capture
domain-specific structure, and Monte Carlo ground truth introduces sampling
error. Negative and heterogeneous subgroup results are retained, and
exploratory oracle comparisons are not selection evidence.
